# Pilot candidate results (review required)

All ten photographs were processed at their original 4032 × 3024 pixels. Four well interiors were located in each photograph. A 12% radius margin excluded the reflective edge.

The revised detector marks 913 compact bright/dark spots and 20 broader dark patches (933 candidate regions total). The broad patch in `01-IMG_0989/W01_candidates.png` corresponds to the dark area pointed out in review. Red outlines mark broad patches. None of these regions is a confirmed colony, and no supervised colony classifier has been trained. A broad dark patch may contain multiple colonies or may reflect staining or illumination.

Three well views in image 08 still have zero candidate regions at the default threshold. Zero means no region passed these settings, not biological absence. Counts vary sharply with the threshold and capture conditions and must not be interpreted as colony forming units, dilution-response measurements, or accuracy. The photographs may be repeated views of the same physical wells.
